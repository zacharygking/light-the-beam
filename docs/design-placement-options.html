Where the scoreboard goes

Three ways to mount the 2.8" ESP32 display board on the 220mm Golden 1 Center print. Each option is drawn twice: what you see from the couch, and the model cut in half so the board, beam strip and wiring are visible. Purple is the beam. Green is the board. Sizes are real: the board is 86 × 50mm and its screen shows 57 × 43mm.

Option A · Recommended

Plinth under the arena

STL work
Design one new plinth (OpenSCAD). Arena prints as downloaded.
Look
Arena on a stand. Screen sits below the building, like a marquee.
Wiring
Board and USB jack live in the plinth. One 3-wire lead goes up through the arena's floor to the beam strip.
Fit risk
Low. The plinth is sized to the board; nothing has to squeeze inside the arena.
Extra print
About 120 g of filament, 4 to 5 hours.
Option B

Window cut into the arena wall

STL work
Add a negative box in Bambu Studio to cut the window. Print a small bezel to cover the rough edge.
Look
Most integrated. Screen sits in the building's front. The curved wall is flattened around it.
Wiring
Board inside the arena next to the beam strip; USB cable leaves through the existing wire hole.
Fit risk
Medium. The board's connectors and the beam stand share a tight cavity; needs one test print of the cut region.
Extra print
None beyond a bezel.
Option C

Separate stand beside it

STL work
Design a small box for the board (OpenSCAD). Arena prints as downloaded.
Look
Two pieces on the shelf. Screen angle is adjustable; a cable runs between them.
Wiring
Board and USB in the box. A short lead crosses to the arena's wire hole to reach the beam strip.
Fit risk
Lowest. Nothing shares space with the arena.
Extra print
About 60 g of filament, 2 hours.

What the screen shows

The three states of the 320 × 240 display, drawn at the real pixel size. Dark ground so it reads like a scoreboard, not a gadget. Big condensed digits, each team in its own color, and a live pill that pulses. Team logos are pulled from ESPN once and baked into the firmware.

Arena base220mm diameter, hollow, roof lifts off. Wire hole in the back, beam hole in the lid.
Display board86 × 50 × 12mm, screen area 57 × 43mm, USB powered.
BeamWS2812B strip, 10 to 20 LEDs, inside a 12mm frosted tube. Purple on a Kings win.
All optionsOne USB cable powers everything. The board fetches scores over WiFi.
